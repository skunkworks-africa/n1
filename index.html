Privacy & access

This public website contains the application interface. Project tasks are requested directly from Microsoft Graph after you sign in and Microsoft checks your plan access.

Task content and access tokens stay in browser memory during your session. This site has no advertising or analytics. Microsoft processes sign-in and Planner requests under your organization’s policies; GitHub hosts the public website.

External partners need an approved guest account and membership in the plan’s group. A successful sign-in does not grant membership. Direct personal Microsoft account access is not supported.
